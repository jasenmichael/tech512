# Service page depth

Notes for the TECH 512 service pages. Sources are the pages themselves, fetched 2026-10-28. Patterns below are what those pages show. They are not claims about TECH 512.

## Miles IT

Source: https://www.milesit.com/lp/custom-software-development/

The page names the job, then problems in the customer's words, then specialties, then four steps (Discovery, Project plan, Rapid quality development, Launch and advance), then cost, FAQs, and a contact close.

Borrowed structure: name the job, say what is inside the engagement, say what is outside it, then point to contact.

Left behind, because that page states them and TECH 512 has no matching fact:

- "99.7% project success rate", "2,000+ projects completed in 30 years", and "100+ full-time software experts".
- The quote from Mara Reuben, President, ParkNSave.
- Specialties beyond an application for one customer: AI software development, SaaS products, and separate lines for databases and portals.
- "Get a Free Project Plan" as its own offer.

## Spud Software

Source: https://spudsoftware.com/

The homepage gets specific by listing what the work covers, who it fits, and who it does not fit (cheapest SaaS, simple off-the-shelf needs, a one-time project with no ongoing relationship).

Borrowed: say what the work covers and what it is not.

Left behind:

- SpudERP as a starting platform, and the module lists under Sales and CRM, Production and Operations, and the other headings on that page.
- "Your software department. Without building one." and the ongoing Software Partnership as the offer.
- The first-90-days sequence: Understand the Business, Prioritize, Configure and Build, Go Live, Continuously Evolve.
- Spud's own facts: no offshore outsourcing, 29+ years, Grand Blanc, Michigan.

## SoftServe

Source: https://www.softserveinc.com/en-us

The homepage leads with named client stories (Montblanc, Ensight, Quavo, Zenoss) and partner tiers (AWS, NVIDIA, Google Cloud, Microsoft). The contact block on that page gives an Austin address: 201 W 5th Street Suite 1550 Austin, TX 78701.

Left behind: client stories and partner tiers. The TECH 512 glossary avoids case studies and client stories, and none are on file. Do not claim that Austin address. TECH 512 is in Elgin.

## RevenueBase Austin list

Source: https://revenuebase.ai/companies/software-engineering-firms/united-states/austin

The page says RevenueBase identified 633 company matches for software engineering firms associated with Austin, United States, as of September 6, 2026. The preview ranks firms by contacts tracked. Recorded descriptions there are generic ("technology services", "custom software development and IT consulting", "digital innovation"). The page says those descriptions are compiled in the RevenueBase company graph and are not guaranteed quotations from the company's website.

Borrowed: a specific offer beats a generic services blurb. The specifics already on the TECH 512 site are Elgin, since 2007, customers anywhere, marketing inside the website, and not a repair shop.

Left behind: treating the list as a service menu, and any claim that TECH 512 is headquartered in Austin.
