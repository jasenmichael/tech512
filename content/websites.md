A website is the public site.

## What the build includes

The work runs in this order: page structure, the pages, the copy, then metadata, sitemap, and on-page SEO.

[What a website includes](/articles/what-a-website-includes) walks through each piece.

## What marketing means

Marketing is the structure and the copy that earn the visit.

## What a website is not

That work is not an ads retainer, and it is not a separate marketing service.

## When the public site is the wrong job

When the job is an application, that is [custom software](/custom-software).
