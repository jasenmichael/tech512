# TECH 512

The language for the TECH 512 website and the work it offers.

## Language

**Business name**:
The public name of the practice.
_Avoid_: TECH512 as the legal name, a second brand

**Customer**:
A person or a business, anywhere, who hires TECH 512.
_Avoid_: account

**Website**:
A public site. Marketing and SEO are part of a Website.
_Avoid_: SEO retainer, marketing agency

**Site findability**:
How searchers find the TECH 512 website.
_Avoid_: on-page SEO inside a customer Website, an SEO offer

**Custom software**:
An application built for one Customer, including web applications, custom apps, automation, and a native app when the project needs one.
_Avoid_: IT service, repair

**Consulting**:
The conversation that starts the work.
_Avoid_: IT consulting, a service page

**Service area**:
The towns listed on the site. It does not limit who can be a Customer.
_Avoid_: shop address, storefront

**Article**:
An explanation published on the site.
_Avoid_: case study, client story
